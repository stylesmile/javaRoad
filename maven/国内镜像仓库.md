## 阿里云
```
<repository>
    <id>aliyun-nexus</id>
    <name>aliyun-nexus</name>
    <url>https://maven.aliyun.com/repository/public</url>
    <snapshots><!--快照-->
        <enabled>false</enabled>
    </snapshots>
    <releases><!--正式版-->
        <enabled>true</enabled>
    </releases>
</repository>
```
