主要三个环境变量
## window
GOROOT=D:\software-program\go\go1.23.12

GOPATH=D:\code\go-project
GOPATH=D:\code\GOPATH

PATH=D:\software-program\go1.23.1\bin


可选

GOPROXY=https://mirrors.aliyun.com/goproxy/
或者
GOPROXY=https://goproxy.cn

## mac
sudo vi /etc/profile
export GOROOT=/Volumes/soft/software/go/go1.24.13
export GOPATH=/Volumes/soft/code/go-project
export GOPATH=/Volumes/soft/code/GOPATH
export PATH=$PATH:/Volumes/soft/software/go/go1.24.13/bin
